Our Story

A Journey of Craftsmanship

Founded in 2015, PatioHaven began with a simple vision: to create outdoor furniture that blends timeless elegance with modern durability. What started as a small workshop has grown into a trusted name in outdoor living.

2015 — The Beginning

Founded with a commitment to quality craftsmanship and sustainable materials.

2018 — Expansion

Opened our first showroom and expanded our collection to include dining and lounge furniture.

2022 — Sustainability Focus

Achieved FSC certification and committed to 100% sustainable sourcing.

2024 — Today

Trusted by thousands of customers for premium outdoor furniture and exceptional service.

Craftsmanship

Where Art Meets Function

Our furniture is not just built—it's crafted. Each piece is carefully designed to create the perfect balance of comfort, durability, and aesthetic appeal. We work with skilled artisans who bring decades of experience to every project.

From the selection of premium teak and rattan to the final finishing touches, every step of our process reflects a deep commitment to quality. We take pride in creating furniture that tells a story and enriches your outdoor living experience.
