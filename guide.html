Seasonal Care Guide

Each season brings unique challenges and opportunities for your outdoor furniture. Here's how to keep your pieces in perfect condition throughout the year.

Spring

Clean thoroughly and inspect for winter damage

Summer

Shade and protect from UV rays; clean regularly

Autumn

Clean leaves and debris; prepare for winter storage

Winter

Store or cover properly; prevent moisture damage

Material Care

Care by Material

Specific care instructions for different materials

Wood

Oil regularly to prevent drying and cracking. Clean with mild soap and water. Avoid standing water.

Teak, Cedar, Pine

Metal

Wipe with damp cloth and dry. Use protective coatings to prevent rust. Inspect for scratches.

Aluminum, Steel

Rattan & Wicker

Brush regularly to prevent dust buildup. Avoid moisture exposure. Use gentle cleaners.

Natural & Synthetic

Fabric & Cushions

Spot clean with mild detergent. Store indoors during harsh weather. Use fabric protector sprays.

Textilene, Sunbrella
